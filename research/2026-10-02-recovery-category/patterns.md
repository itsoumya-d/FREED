## Home / Today

Order the screen around what matters immediately:

1. A slim protection state row using current native state. Text plus symbol: Active, Needs attention, Temporarily unlocked, Checking or Unavailable. Action opens Shield/setup as appropriate. On iOS distinguish Safari adult-domain blocking, selected-app shielding and Safari short-form rules. Do not imply that adult content inside all apps is scanned.
2. A spacious recovery hero: one dominant day count (44–56 pt, tabular); its label (15–17 pt); truthful elapsed hours/minutes beneath; next milestone as a quiet line with compact progress. Keep the start-date meaning consistent with recovery state. A zero-day user should still see meaningful elapsed time. A slip updates this hero while preserving their best.
3. Exactly one next action. If a reset is saved, show its title, remaining work and Continue saved reset. Otherwise show today's check-in, then pledge if still available, then a small suggested practice. Do not repeat a large CTA for every possible action.
4. Today's practices in one compact grouped list, section count only, checkmarks and a short completion response. Three visible rows plus remaining items is preferable to six tall standalone cards. All six existing habits remain reachable.
5. Quick tools: Breathe, Start a reset, CLARA and Library. Prefer a small grid or compact list. SOS stays accessible in the native root header and must bypass ads.

At a normal phone size, initial viewport should contain protection state, recovery hero, next action and the start of daily practices. Move extended activity estimates, XP/level, best streak, extra snapshots and duplicated 90-day progress to Progress. Do not repeat the day count in a metric tile, countdown card, goal card and milestone card on Home.

## Shield

Expose useful controls before diagnostics. First current status and scope, then selected apps/daily limit, then schedules, web safety and allow list. Group the operational rows (icon, title, value/status, chevron/toggle) instead of stacking explanatory cards before controls. Keep DNS health, rule counts, checksums, calibration internals and URL diagnostics in an explicit Advanced section. Explain one limitation alongside the relevant control. A whole-screen technical warning pile makes the screen feel broken even when only one permission needs attention.

References: Opal 1497465230/oth_313tk, oth_90b92, oth_zekjp; BePresent 1644737181/oth_1v56k and oth_irbex; one sec 1532875441/oth_eml06 and oth_oi13r. Status language must follow FREED native truth, not these apps' screenshots.

## Progress

Show real history first: current/best streak and seven-day recorded activity; then check-in patterns and reset outcomes; then milestones/XP; then deeper reports. Use numbers from logs. An empty state is a composed invitation to record the first check-in, not an empty ring alongside synthetic scores. Activity formulas may be retained only as clearly described estimates; avoid measured-looking Clarity/Energy/Sleep Guard labels inferred from mood/sleep answers. Suppress forecasts with low confidence, and show which dates/counts a summary uses.

References: I Am Sober 672904239/oth_x7ykp, oth_vgmbm, oth_t0z2n; one sec 1532875441/oth_m3svb; BePresent 1644737181/oth_s0kn0. FREED should not adopt competitor score formulas or clinical promises.

## Urge / reset flow

Urgent content needs less reading: heading, one calm instruction, one primary reset action; secondary Breathe or Message partner; Close/Back to safety always clear. Put block details behind an explanatory row. Show actual challenge steps, time or measured requirement, pause/resume and permission alternatives. Completion feedback is separate from verification: ask whether the urge passed before counting helpful outcome. An interrupted reset should resume with honest active time.

References: Opal 1497465230/oth_7bdfy (clear reason and dismiss), oth_kescp and oth_bxcas (focused active state/leave decision); BePresent 1644737181/oth_pmj7g and oth_jhl03 (visually distinct session/break states); I Am Sober 672904239/oth_5p5ku (guided tool explanation). These stills do not prove the transitions.

Remove observed source copy such as automatic “mind cleared,” guaranteed urge weakening, nervous-system restoration after a button hold and exact 180-second urge-peak prediction. Breathing copy should describe the cadence, not promise autonomic/brain effects. A three-second hold is an interaction, not a verified recovery victory. Do not record a success merely because a decorative countdown ended.

## Profile / library / forms

Profile: recognizable account/membership header followed by grouped sections for reminders, recovery history, accountability, backup, privacy and support. Keep long backup/partner forms inside their chosen section rather than all expanded by default. Retain every feature and dangerous-action explanation; destruction gets its own confirmation.

Library: tools by intent first, reading content second. Use one featured recommendation, compact utility rows and article category/duration. Article body uses readable 16–17 pt text and 24–26 pt leading. Do not animate every paragraph while reading. Forms use explicit labels, selected state with checkmark/radio, low visual decoration, keyboard-visible Save and preserved draft.

## Motion and verification

Native tab/push/back grammar remains the default. Rows highlight; buttons press subtly; saved/completed states use a small opacity/change response. No repeated haptics in timers or 100 ms hold loops. Breathing phase motion is meaningful but must have Reduce Motion/text cadence alternatives. Layout should survive both themes, XL text, keyboard and compact width. Reference screenshots cannot verify these states: parent must capture the final FREED flow and motion on devices.
