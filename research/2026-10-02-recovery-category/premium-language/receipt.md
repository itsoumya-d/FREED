# Additional premium-language evidence — 2026-10-02

Purpose: define FREED's original premium language after the user's explicit Opal + QUITTR direction. This extends the earlier task-specific study; already inspected QUITTR/product media was reused.

New source: Appllama list_app_screens for Opal 1497465230, section onboarding; sequential page cursors. All 29 entries saved: 28 stills inspected in four contact boards and one 215.6-second video inspected through 27 keyframes at 8-second intervals. Existing QUITTR recording remains in the parent research folder. No browser, Simulator, app/source changes, account entry, purchase, external message or deployment were performed.

The recording supports visual-anchor continuity, current-question hierarchy, keyboard/auth/system-permission boundaries and inline selection recovery. Its lifetime-use/savings and health claims were identified as content that FREED should not import. Sampled frames do not establish transition durations, native gestures or frame rate. The design document's motion timings are proposed FREED rules.

[Design language](../../../docs/design/freed-design-language.md) contains the implementation target and all 22 surfaces. It preserves the approved paywall and native tabs. All image/video files were downloaded while links were live; durable IDs survive link expiry. Competitor media is reference material, not FREED production art.
