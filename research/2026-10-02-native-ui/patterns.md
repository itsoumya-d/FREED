# Native FREED research

Studied 37 task-specific Appllama images on 2026-10-02, plus HabitKit and Opal references from the planning pass. Durable IDs and image provenance are in screens.json; seven boards provide visual comparison. Appllama watermarks are provenance and are not app assets.

Adopted patterns: four system-backed tab peers, per-tab push navigation, grouped settings rows with visible state, one clear daily next action, sparse habit progress, explicit permission recovery, guided check-in selection, verifiable exercise requirements, and quiet success feedback. Keep FREED's mint accent and cool neutral semantic surfaces. Do not reproduce competitor artwork, claims, score formulas or ornate timers. Paywall remains unchanged.

Motion: system tab/push gestures; brief press feedback; small completion/state fades; breathing cadence is explanatory. Reduce Motion removes spatial effects. Screen recordings, both themes, large text and real device performance are separate verification requirements.
