# Setup and membership redesign

Studied 39 Appllama images across screen-time permission flows and membership screens. Source app/screen IDs and downloaded references are in references.json; the four contact sheets record the reviewed set.

- ScreenZen and Opal show one decision, a brief purpose, a clear next action and progress. System authorization remains the actual permission boundary.
- ScreenZen's picker follows permission approval immediately. Optional notification prompts stay separate from essential app protection.
- HabitKit uses selectable plan rows, readable totals and quiet restore; Calo separates total and cadence clearly; MealPrepPro keeps a single purchase action.
- FREED retains its mint accent, uses semantic light/dark surfaces and large touch targets. Required background configuration shares a visible stage with the associated permission instead of creating extra prompts.
- Membership has two equal-benefit plans, actual store prices, no trial, no fabricated social proof, and one-tap dismissal. Purchasing and restoring retain the existing verified-entitlement controller.

Verification must cover both themes, setup denial/return/retry, unavailable native capabilities, loading/error pricing, and simulator navigation. Physical-device authorization and store checkout evidence remain separate launch requirements.
