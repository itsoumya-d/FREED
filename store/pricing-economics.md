# FREED launch pricing

The approved launch catalog contains two subscriptions with identical Premium benefits and no trial.

| Plan | Product ID | US price | Interval |
| --- | --- | ---: | --- |
| Monthly | `freed_premium_monthly` | US$9.99 | Month |
| Annual | `freed_premium_yearly` | US$39.99 | Year |

The annual US price is approximately US$3.33 per month equivalent. Native store prices, currency, taxes and renewal terms shown at checkout govern each purchase. Actual proceeds come from the store financial reports.

Checkout uses native StoreKit/Play Billing and authoritative Firebase verification. Cancellation keeps Premium through the paid period; expiry, revocation, refund and payment hold restore eligible free-user ads. Urgent SOS remains ad-free. Additional tiers and a lifetime product are outside this launch.

Store fee assumptions and account eligibility must be checked separately in the official [Apple App Store Small Business Program](https://developer.apple.com/app-store/small-business-program/) and [Google Play service fees](https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/112622). No financial-return guarantee is made.
