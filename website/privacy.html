FREED Privacy Policy

FREED provides recovery and digital self-control tools. This policy explains the data used by the app and its optional services.

Email privacy supportAccount deletion

Eligibility and account

FREED is for adults 18+ and voluntary personal use. A genuine Apple, Google or email account is required. Do not use FREED to monitor another person without their knowledge and consent.

Sign-in services process the identity and authentication information needed to connect your account. FREED uses your account identity to protect account operations, optional hosted features and purchase ownership; local progress does not substitute for a genuine account.

Data FREED uses on device

Recovery history, check-ins, journal entries, drafts, challenge progress, settings, reminders and protection selections are stored locally. FREED also stores selected Android package IDs or opaque Apple tokens, domain-feed version/checksum and verified entitlement state. Challenge selection and retention planning run locally; they do not send recovery context to a remote challenge or retention service.

Detect openings of selected apps to apply your whole-app guards. You choose whether to open FREED recovery. Native Shorts and Reels inspection is experimental and outside the public release.

FREED uses Usage Access only to read aggregate foreground time for selected app packages so your app timers can trigger recovery challenges. It does not read screen contents, messages, keystrokes, browsing history, or raw usage events.

Android DNS-only VPN protection, called DNS Guard, classifies DNS questions locally against the adult-domain feed. Normal browsing stays direct. FREED does not full-tunnel traffic, inspect non-DNS packet contents, proxy browsing or decrypt HTTPS. Another VPN, encrypted DNS, unsupported browsers, network conditions and operating-system restrictions can limit filtering; no tool blocks every adult-content source.

On iOS, Family Controls, ManagedSettings, DeviceActivity and Safari Content Blocker apply selected whole-app and supported website restrictions after authorization. Apple Screen Time selections remain opaque tokens and counts; FREED does not receive a full app or browsing history.

Supported protection notices offer a user-initiated recovery handoff: you choose whether to open FREED. Required permissions, capabilities and activation checks vary by platform and installed build. A saved setting alone does not prove active enforcement.

Photos used for supported exercise checks are processed on your device with Apple Vision or bundled ML Kit. Photos are not uploaded. ML Kit may send operational SDK metrics. Availability depends on the installed build and supported exercise.

Optional Google CLARA messages

With your explicit cloud-chat consent, messages you deliberately send to CLARA are processed by Google through FREED. If you also choose to share conversation context, the request can include up to six recent user-authored messages. The app redacts supported sensitive patterns, but do not type information you do not want processed by the service.

Browsing activity, journal entries, photos and Screen Time data are not automatically attached. Challenge selection and retention planning remain local. Processing may happen outside your country. Google applies its service processing and retention terms; these can include provider-side retention and are separate from deleting local chat history.

CLARA uses Google Cloud’s Gemini service. Read Google’s Gemini processing and retention documentation. Provider retention depends on the applicable service terms and configuration; FREED does not promise a fixed Google retention period or zero retention.

FREED's backend does not persist chat prompts or full provider results. It keeps account-linked quota and request deduplication metadata to enforce service limits and prevent repeated requests. Request deduplication records have a configured 7-day expiry. Expiry makes records eligible for deletion; physical deletion may occur later. These service controls do not set Google's content retention.

Turning cloud chat off stops future messages; an already sent request may finish. Built-in guidance remains available when consent is off or the service is unavailable. Cloud chat is subject to service availability, quotas and server controls.

Optional backup and analytics

If you enable hosted encrypted backup, FREED sends encrypted backup envelopes. FREED does not receive your passphrase. Keep your passphrase and any exported copies secure; deleting this device's data does not erase external exports.

If you opt into analytics sharing, FREED sends absolute UTC-day counts of check-ins and completed challenges for a 14-day window. The snapshot also includes schema and consent versions, opt-in time, and lineage, revision and snapshot identifiers. These records are linked to your authenticated account UID. Locally derived rates, streak summaries and challenge categories are not part of this uploaded snapshot. Raw URLs, search text, journal notes, exact selected Apple tokens and exact coordinates are excluded.

Payments, ads and permissions

Current Premium plans are auto-renewing monthly and yearly subscriptions with the same features. Apple App Store or Google Play handles billing. FREED grants Premium only after store purchase and account ownership verification. Prices, billing periods and renewal terms appear in the store purchase flow.

Purchase verification sends store transaction metadata to FREED's server to verify access and ownership. FREED does not receive full payment card details.

Basic Help/SOS is included with free access and requires neither a paid subscription nor a rewarded ad. Basic Help/SOS and the native protection barrier are ad-free. Free users may explicitly choose a rewarded ad for access to one guided recovery session where available. An ad or challenge never releases baseline adult-domain protection. Sensitive recovery data is not used for ad targeting.

Ad requests use non-personalized options where supported and applicable consent controls. Advertising and photo SDKs may process operational data under their own terms; on-device photo processing does not mean every SDK is network-free.

Permissions are requested for matching features: VPN/DNS Guard, Accessibility and Usage Access on Android; Screen Time and Safari restrictions on iOS; and camera, activity, foreground location or notifications for supported exercises or reminders. FREED cannot silently grant operating-system permissions.

Retention, deletion and contact

Analytics records have a configured 14-day expiry. Day counters expire 14 days after their UTC-day boundary; snapshot and receipt metadata expire 14 days after their write. Expiry makes records eligible for TTL deletion; physical deletion may occur later and is not guaranteed at the exact expiry time.

Local recovery data remains on your device unless you enable an optional service or deliberately share a message. Server service records, aggregate analytics and encrypted backups follow their configured retention and deletion processes; legally required purchase or security records may be retained. No immediate provider-erasure guarantee is implied.

Use Delete Local Data for this device or Delete Account & Data for an account deletion request. See the account deletion page for native acknowledgment, retries, retained records and separate subscription cancellation.
