FAQ

Straight answers

Will DNS Guard slow down my internet or YouTube?

No. The filter routes only DNS name lookups — your video, streaming, and download traffic never passes through it. Allowed lookups are forwarded to fast public resolvers (1.1.1.1 / 1.0.0.1) with automatic failover.

Does FREED block only adult sites?

Yes. Blocking is default-allow: only domains on the reviewed adult list, explicit adult search intent, and the apps you personally select are interrupted. Everything else — work, study, streaming — is untouched, and you can add always-allow domains yourself.

Do I need an account?

No. FREED works fully offline-first without any account. An optional account only adds encrypted backup and cross-device restore, and you can skip it entirely.

What if I use another VPN?

Android allows one VPN at a time, so DNS Guard pauses while another VPN is active and FREED tells you honestly in Shield health. iOS protection uses Screen Time and Safari, which coexist with any VPN.

How much does Premium cost?

Premium is $39.99 per year (the equivalent of $3.33 per month — 67% off the monthly rate) or $9.99 per month. Both include every premium feature. Free covers the core blocker, shields, and challenges forever.

How do I delete my data?

Profile → Delete Local Data wipes everything on-device instantly. Hosted data (if you enabled backup) can be deleted via the account deletion page.
