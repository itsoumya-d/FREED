FREED Account Deletion

Choose a device reset or an account deletion request. This page explains the separate steps, confirmation and retry boundaries.

Email deletion requestPrivacy policySupport

Delete local app data

In Profile, choose Delete Local Data and confirm. FREED first asks the native protection layer to reset DNS Guard, app guard configurations, schedules and temporary unlocks. Recovery records are preserved when native cleanup cannot be acknowledged; retry the reset rather than assuming it succeeded.

After native acknowledgment, the app clears local recovery history, drafts, guided progress and local cloud-chat consent, and disables automatic backup. Storage errors may leave cleanup unfinished; follow the displayed retry instructions. This device reset does not delete the signed-in account, hosted backups or copies you exported. System permissions remain controlled by iOS or Android Settings.

Deleting local data or your account does not cancel App Store or Google Play subscriptions. Cancel separately in the purchasing store account.

Request hosted data deletion

In Profile, choose Delete Account & Data, confirm the signed-in account and complete any required recent sign-in or Apple confirmation. FREED requests server-side account and account-linked data deletion before clearing this device and signing out.

An accepted request means deletion is in progress, not that every server or provider copy has already been erased. If the request is rejected or interrupted, retry or contact support. If account deletion is accepted but device cleanup fails, return to Profile and use Delete Local Data.

The request covers the account and associated hosted recovery backups, optional analytics, push registrations, service records and purchase ownership data according to the service's deletion process. Some purchase, fraud-prevention, security, tax, refund or chargeback records may require legal retention. External store and Google processing records follow their own terms. FREED does not promise instantaneous provider authorization revocation or complete erasure of all retained copies.

For access problems or a deletion request, email soumyadebnath1661@gmail.com from the account email where possible. Include your platform and app version. Support may request identity verification; never send passwords, raw receipts, purchase tokens or private recovery notes.

Subscription cancellation and external copies

Cancel in the store account that purchased the subscription to stop future renewal. Cancellation does not immediately end verified paid access: it continues through the verified paid expiry, subject to refunds, revocation and current verification. Use Restore purchases with the original store account and the owning FREED account; use Manage subscription for the store's current renewal, cancellation and refund information.

Deleting local data, hosted backups or an account does not erase files you exported or messages already processed by Google. Retained external records and store purchases follow the relevant provider's policies.

Public deletion URL and support

The public account deletion URL is https://freed-landing.web.app/account-deletion. This page supports deletion requests even when you cannot access the app.
