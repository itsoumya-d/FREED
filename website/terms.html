What FREED is and who may use it

FREED is for adults 18+ and voluntary personal use. A genuine Apple, Google or email account is required. Do not use FREED to monitor another person without their knowledge and consent.

FREED offers self-control tools, selected whole-app guards, supported website filtering, voluntary recovery exercises and habit tracking. It is not a medical device, does not provide medical advice and is not a substitute for professional treatment. It makes no promise of medical efficacy, a fixed craving duration or complete prevention of adult-content access.

Protection and voluntary recovery

Detect openings of selected apps to apply your whole-app guards. You choose whether to open FREED recovery. Native Shorts and Reels inspection is experimental and outside the public release.

FREED uses Usage Access only to read aggregate foreground time for selected app packages so your app timers can trigger recovery challenges. It does not read screen contents, messages, keystrokes, browsing history, or raw usage events.

Android DNS-only VPN protection, called DNS Guard, classifies DNS questions locally against the adult-domain feed. Normal browsing stays direct. FREED does not full-tunnel traffic, inspect non-DNS packet contents, proxy browsing or decrypt HTTPS. Another VPN, encrypted DNS, unsupported browsers, network conditions and operating-system restrictions can limit filtering; no tool blocks every adult-content source.

On iOS, Family Controls, ManagedSettings, DeviceActivity and Safari Content Blocker apply selected whole-app and supported website restrictions after authorization. Apple Screen Time selections remain opaque tokens and counts; FREED does not receive a full app or browsing history.

Supported protection notices offer a user-initiated recovery handoff: you choose whether to open FREED. Required permissions, capabilities and activation checks vary by platform and installed build. A saved setting alone does not prove active enforcement.

Choose exercises appropriate to your abilities and circumstances, including gentle alternatives. Stop if an activity causes pain or discomfort. Strenuous exercise is not required for basic help. Ads and challenge completion never turn off baseline adult-domain protection.

Cancellation does not immediately end verified paid access: it continues through the verified paid expiry, subject to refunds, revocation and current verification. Use Restore purchases with the original store account and the owning FREED account; use Manage subscription for the store's current renewal, cancellation and refund information.

Basic Help/SOS is included with free access and requires neither a paid subscription nor a rewarded ad. Basic Help/SOS and the native protection barrier are ad-free. Free users may explicitly choose a rewarded ad for access to one guided recovery session where available. An ad or challenge never releases baseline adult-domain protection. Renewal, cancellation deadlines and refunds follow the purchasing store's displayed terms and policies. Current subscriptions do not change existing verified purchase ownership or historical recovery records.
