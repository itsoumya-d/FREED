# Adult-domain artifact inputs — 16 October 2026

This is primary-source desk research for the explicit feed input missing from Task94. It is not a downloaded, reviewed, packaged or deployed corpus. No feed, domain traffic, DNS setting, app protection mode, server or paid service was changed.

[OISD's official list description](https://oisd.nl/downloads) separates NSFW and NSFW Small from its advertising/malware lists. Its [official FAQ](https://oisd.nl/faq) identifies NSFW Small as NSFW domains in Tranco's top-million set, explains unsupported-size/truncation risks and states GPLv3 distribution for OISD lists. Therefore the existing source-family label alone is insufficient provenance for a FREED release artifact: record the exact input bytes/version, upstream license and notices, chosen scope, validation and packaging obligations before shipping. No OISD corpus was fetched here. OISD Small and NSFW Small are distinct; using the ad-blocking list is not a substitute for adult categories and can interfere with rewarded-ad delivery.

The [StevenBlack official category readme](https://github.com/StevenBlack/hosts/blob/master/alternates/porn-only/readme.md) describes a category-only variant; the [repository license](https://github.com/StevenBlack/hosts/blob/master/license.txt) is MIT and requires its notice. This is an aggregator with contributing-source metadata, not permission to assume every upstream term or category outcome is reviewed. Its current readme reports a corpus far above Task94's 1,000-domain internal source bound. No production feed is selected from it by this research, and no arbitrary first-1,000 truncation is approved.

Task94 remains explicit-input, SHA256-pinned and internal/default-off. Its 1MiB/1,000 bounds are conservative source constraints, not supported Safari capacity or comprehensive adult coverage. A synthetic reviewed fixture exercises compilation; it does not become a real reviewed feed. A future actual corpus needs its own content/license/provenance review and the feed checksum/version bound to the exact rules/permissions/package. Browser install/grants, precedence over existing layers, ordinary browsing, recovery entry and no-SafeSearch migration still require separate native/device evidence.

The existing Families baseline stays in place. No account, purchase, API subscription or new paid infrastructure is necessary for this desk-research step.
